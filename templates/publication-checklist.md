The package remains private unless a responsible human completes a structured
promotion record and the maintained validator accepts it. A
`public-candidate` package is not publication or release approval.

- [ ] Scope and exact revisions are recorded.
- [ ] Private and restricted evidence is identified.
- [ ] Public queries contain no private identifiers.
- [ ] Redistribution status is recorded for every external source.
- [ ] Threat and review claims do not exceed their evidence.
- [ ] Generated diagrams are current.
- [ ] Responsible-human promotion record is complete when promotion is requested.
